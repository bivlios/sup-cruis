Prototype feedback

Thank you for testing the SUP Cruise business manager. Honest comments will help make the final app simple and useful.

Suggested trial

  1. Add a new enquiry, a follow-up date and mark whether the customer is new or returning.
  2. Open the client card, create a client quote and try the copy or email action.
  3. Record a deposit and check the remaining balance.
  4. Add two supplier quotes for the same cruise, select one and turn it into an expense.
  5. Attach one invoice to a client and another to a supplier.
  6. Open a cruise card and check its clients, guests, crew and selected suppliers.
  7. Add an exhibition website and a task with a deadline.
  8. Check the calendar and reminder centre, then repeat one or two actions on your phone.
Please use fictional information only. Do not enter real passport numbers, customer details or payment references during this prototype test. This form does not send anything automatically.
